Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for job seekers, recruiters, and companies — so you can get started with confidence.

Click User Login in the top menu, then choose signup / register. Fill in your personal details, upload a profile photo if available, and submit the form. After registration, log in with your email and password to browse and apply for jobs.
Log in as a job seeker, open Jobs List / Latest Jobs, and select a role that matches your skills. Open the job details page and click Apply. Make sure your profile information is complete before applying.
After you apply, recruiters and companies review your profile and update the application status (for example pending, accepted, or rejected). Keep your contact details updated so you can be reached for interviews.
Go to Recruiter Login and complete the signup form with your company details. New recruiter accounts are reviewed by the company/admin team. Once your status is accepted, you can log in and start managing jobs and candidates.
After logging in, open Add Job from the navbar. Enter the job title, category, sub category, location, experience, salary, and description, then submit. You can later update or manage posts from Manage Job.
Companies can create an account, manage recruiters (pending / accepted / rejected), post and manage jobs, review candidate applications, and view registered users — all from the company dashboard after login.
On the login page, use the Forgot Password option. Enter your registered email, verify the OTP sent to you, and set a new password. After a successful reset, log in with your new credentials. Logged-in users can also update their password from the profile menu.
Use the sun / moon toggle on the right side of the top navigation bar. Your preference is saved in the browser so it stays active as you move between pages.
Visit the Contact page for phone, email, and support timings, or use the email form in the website footer. Our team is available to help with account, job posting, and application questions.
